Contact and support

We aim to reply within two working days.

In shortEmail [contact email] for anything below. Put the topic in the subject line so we can route it quickly.

1. Report a suspicious listing

Nia and the opportunities we publish never ask you to pay to apply. If a listing or message asks you for money, or looks fake, email us with the listing title and any screenshots. Use the subject Report listing. Please do not pay anything in the meantime.

2. Your account and data

You can edit your details, download your data or delete your account under Profile, then Settings. If you cannot sign in or need help with a data request, email us with the subject Account help. For security we may ask you to confirm it is you.

3. Technical help

If something is not working, tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and which device and browser you use. Use the subject Technical help.

  • Did not get your verification code? Check your spam folder, then use Resend code on the sign-up screen.
  • Forgot your password? Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in form.

4. Employers and partners

If your organisation would like to list jobs, internships or attachments on Nia, email us with the subject Partnership.
