FAQ

Common questions

What does Nia cost?

Creating an account, building your profile and browsing opportunities are free. Applying to opportunities needs a Nia membership of KES 250 per month, paid by M-Pesa or card through Paystack. We never charge you automatically.

Who can apply?

Kenyan students and graduates looking for jobs, internships or attachments.

What details will employers see?

Only what you submit with an application, such as your CV and the contact details the opportunity asks for.

I forgot my password.

Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in form and we will email you a reset link.
