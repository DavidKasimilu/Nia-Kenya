Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

In shortWe collect only what we need to match you with opportunities: your account details, profile and CV, and what you submit when you apply. We do not sell your data. You can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Nia Kenya (“Nia”, “we”, “us”) runs a web platform that helps students and graduates in Kenya find jobs, internships and industrial attachments. For the purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2019, Nia is the data controller of the personal data described here.

Contact: [contact email] · [postal address]

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.
  • Profile details you add: full name, phone number, WhatsApp number, county of residence, institution, course, a short bio and a profile photo.
  • Your CV (PDF, DOC or DOCX) if you upload one.
  • Applications: which opportunities you apply to, and the extra details an opportunity asks for, such as availability, preferred contact method or admission number.
  • Account status and activity: sign-up date, application status, and records of administrative actions such as account suspension.
  • Technical data: your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay logged in. Our hosting and network providers process your IP address and basic request data to deliver and protect the service.

You choose what to put in your profile. Please do not upload documents or details that an opportunity does not need, such as national ID numbers, unless we ask for them.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your profile and CV to our team, and to employers where needed, when you apply.
  • To contact you about your applications, such as a status update or an invitation to an interview.
  • To run, protect and improve the platform, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep an audit trail of administrative actions.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

4. Our legal basis

We process your data on these grounds under the Data Protection Act, 2019: your consent (for example when you create an account and upload a CV, which you can withdraw), performance of our service to you (running your account and applications), our legitimate interests in keeping the platform secure and working, and legal obligations where they apply.

5. Who we share it with

  • Nia administrators and moderators who review applications. Access is limited to people who need it to do their job.
  • Employers and organisations that offer an opportunity. If you apply to a listing offered by a third party, we may share the details you submitted for that application, including your CV, so they can assess and contact you.
  • Service providers that run the platform for us: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), Cloudflare and our web hosting provider (delivery and security). These providers act on our instructions.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising. Your browser also loads fonts and code libraries from third-party content delivery networks (such as Google Fonts and jsDelivr), which may see your IP address when they do.

6. Where your data is stored

Our service providers may store and process data on servers outside Kenya. Where that happens, we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards, as the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires for transfers of personal data out of Kenya.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account or ask us to, we delete your profile, CV and applications, except for limited records we must or need to keep, such as security and audit logs, or data we are legally required to retain.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to or restrict processing of your data;
  • correct inaccurate or outdated data (you can edit most of it in your profile);
  • ask us to delete your data; and
  • receive your data in a portable format, where applicable.

You can download your data or delete your account yourself under Profile, then Settings. For anything else, email [contact email]. We will respond within the time the law allows. We may need to confirm your identity first.

9. How we protect your data

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), database access rules that restrict each account to its own data, and private file storage for CVs, which are opened only through short-lived links. Administrative access is limited and logged. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

10. Cookies and local storage

Nia uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in. This is essential to the service. We do not currently use advertising or analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

11. Age

Nia is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete the account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

13. Contact and complaints

Questions or requests: [contact email]. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.
